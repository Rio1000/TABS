Help for the TABS website and iPhone app · Updated November 24, 2026

Contact us

Questions, problems, account requests or safety concerns: email the developer directly.

support@tabsonfriends.com

We reply within 2 business days, usually sooner. Reports of harassment or abuse are reviewed within 24 hours.

To help us help you, tell us whether you use the iPhone app or the website, your device and iOS or browser version, what you were doing and what happened. A screenshot helps. Never send us your password.

Frequently asked questions

Your account

How do I delete my account?
  1. Open the menu (the "..." button) and tap Profile.
  2. Tap the Account tab, then Delete Account.
  3. Tap Delete to confirm. If you signed in with Apple, confirm with Apple when asked.

Your profile, tabs, notes, friends, requests, notifications, history, settings and payment handles are deleted right away, and so is your sign-in account. This can't be undone. If you signed in with Apple, TABS's access to your Apple ID is revoked too. See the Privacy Policy for the few things that remain.

Can't sign in? Email us from the address on your account and we'll delete it for you.

Can I get a copy of my data?

Yes. Go to Profile → Account → Export Data. You get a JSON file with everything stored under your account.

  • On the website, Export Data downloads the file.
  • In the iPhone app, it opens the share sheet so you can save the file to Files or send it.

You can also email us and we'll send you a copy.

What's the difference between Disable Account and Delete Account?

Disable Account stops anyone, including you, from signing in, but keeps your data. Delete Account erases it. To turn a disabled account back on, or to delete it, email us from the address on the account.

I can't sign in
  • Email and password: tap Reset Password on the Login screen. We'll email you a reset link; check your spam folder if it doesn't arrive.
  • Google: tap Sign In with Google and choose the same Google account you used before.
  • Apple: tap Sign in with Apple and use the same Apple ID. If you chose "Hide My Email", your account is tied to an Apple relay address, not your real email.
  • Use the same sign-in method you created your account with. Signing in another way can create a separate, empty account.
  • Still stuck? Email us and say which method you used to sign up.

Reminders and notifications

How do reminders work?

Reminders work for people on your Friends list. On a friend's tab, tap the remind button and then Send Reminder. Your friend gets a notification on their devices (if they've turned notifications on) and a message in their TABS notifications.

The reminder notification is written by TABS from a fixed set of friendly messages and filled in with your friend's first name and the amount. There's no box to type your own reminder text, and you can remind the same friend at most once a minute.

Auto reminders don't send anything by themselves. When one is due, TABS puts a "Remind now" note in your own notifications, and you decide whether to send it.

How do I mute reminders or notifications?
  • In TABS, go to Profile → Account → Manage Notifications and turn off Payment reminders, Friend activity, or Enable notifications for everything. Muted types stop arriving on your devices and are hidden from your TABS notifications too; turn them back on to see them again.
  • To stop notifications on your lock screen completely, open iPhone Settings → Notifications → TABS and turn off Allow Notifications. On the website, turn notifications off in your browser's site settings.
  • To stop one person entirely, block them (see below).

Safety

How do I block or report someone?

You can block or report a person from three places:

  • their entry in your Friends list (the same menu that has Remove friend),
  • a pending friend request from them,
  • a reminder notification they sent you.

Block removes them from your friends, clears friend requests between you, and stops them from sending you new requests, reminders or notifications. They are not told: a friend request they try to send you looks sent on their side but never reaches you.

Report lets you pick a reason and add details, and blocks them too unless you untick that box. We review every report within 24 hours and may suspend accounts that break our Terms. The reported person isn't told who reported them.

To unblock someone, open Blocked users in your Profile and tap Unblock.

If you are ever in danger, contact your local emergency services first.

Money and payments

Does TABS move or hold money?

No. TABS only keeps a record of who owes what. It never moves, holds or collects money, and it never sees bank or card details. What people owe each other is between them; TABS isn't part of it.

How do payment links work?
  1. Go to Profile → Account → Payment Requests, turn it on, and add at least one of your Venmo username, PayPal.Me username or Cash App $cashtag.
  2. On a tab where someone owes you, tap the request button. TABS copies a link to your Venmo, PayPal or Cash App page (with the amount when possible) for you to send them. If a friend has shared their Venmo username, you can also open Venmo with a charge request to them.

The payment itself happens in Venmo, PayPal or Cash App. Confirmed friends can see the handles you add; remove them any time in the same screen.

What is "Buy us a coffee"?

An optional tip that helps keep TABS free. It unlocks nothing. In the iPhone app it is an in-app purchase handled by Apple; for refunds, use Apple's Report a Problem page. On the website it goes through Buy Me a Coffee.

Problems and feedback

How do I report a bug or suggest a feature?

Use the Feedback form (also in the TABS menu under Feedback), or email support@tabsonfriends.com with the details listed at the top of this page.

The app says it can't reach TABS

The iPhone app loads TABS from tabsonfriends.com, so it needs an internet connection. Check Wi-Fi or mobile data and tap Retry. If it keeps happening on a working connection, email us.
