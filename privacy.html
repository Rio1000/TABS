Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026 · Last updated November 24, 2026

TABS ("tabs on friends") is an IOU tracker made and run by Parker Heikes ("we", "us"). It is available as a website at tabsonfriends.com and as an iPhone app, which shows the same website and adds native sign-in, notifications, ads and an optional tip. This policy covers both. It explains what we collect, why, who can see it, which companies process it for us, and how to export or delete it.

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • TABS never moves or holds money and never sees your bank or card details.
  • Your list of tabs is private to your account. Friends only see your name, friend code, phone number (if you gave one) and any payment handles you choose to add.
  • You can export or permanently delete your data yourself, at any time, from Profile → Account.

1. What we collect and how

Almost everything TABS stores is something you type in or a choice you make in the app. We store it in Google Firebase under your account.

Account details

  • Email sign-up: first name, last name, phone number and email address. Your password is handled by Firebase Authentication and stored by Google in hashed form; we never see it.
  • Sign in with Google: the name and email address on your Google account.
  • Sign in with Apple: your name (Apple shares it only the first time, and only if you choose to) and an email address, which may be an Apple private relay address if you pick "Hide My Email". Apple also gives us a sign-in token and an authorization code, which are used only to sign you in and, when you delete your account, to revoke TABS's access to your Apple ID.
  • Sign-in records: Firebase Authentication keeps your account creation time and last sign-in time, which you can see under Profile → Stats.

Friend code and friends

  • A random 8-character friend code we generate for you. It is stored in a lookup table with your first and last name so that someone you give the code to can find you.
  • Your friends list and friend requests (sent and received), including each friend's name, friend code, user ID and phone number.

Your tabs

  • The people you track (they do not need to use TABS), amounts, items, notes, who owes whom, interest settings, your running totals, and when you last cleared your list.
  • You decide what goes into a tab. Please don't put sensitive information about yourself or anyone else in notes.

Payment handles

  • If you set up Payment Requests: your Venmo username, PayPal.Me username and/or Cash App $cashtag, and whether the feature is on. TABS uses them only to build links that open those apps. We never receive account numbers, balances or card details.

Reminders and notifications

  • Your in-app notifications (friend requests, accepted requests, payment reminders), each with the sender's user ID, the message and the time.
  • Auto-reminder settings for each friend: on/off, how often, and when one was last sent.
  • Reminder and friend-request notifications are written by our server, not by the sender's app. Reminders use a fixed list of friendly messages, filled in with the recipient's first name and the amount, and the only other personal detail they include is the sender's own name. The TABS app has no way to type your own reminder text.

Device push tokens

  • If you allow notifications, we store a push token for each phone or browser so we can deliver notifications to it. Tokens that stop working are removed automatically.

Safety: blocks and reports

  • People you block: their name and when you blocked them.
  • If you report someone: your user ID and name, their user ID and name, the reason, any details you write, the time, and as evidence up to the 10 most recent reminders, friend requests and request acceptances that person sent you through TABS (message text and time only).
  • A short delivery log: for each sender and recipient, our server keeps the last 20 reminder, friend-request and request-accepted messages it delivered (type, text and time) so reports can be checked. Only our server can read it.
  • Short rate-limit records (two user IDs and a timestamp) that stop people from spamming reminders, friend requests or reports.

Activity and settings

  • An account history log of actions you take (for example "Sent a reminder to Sam"). Only you can see it, under Profile → Account → View Account History, and you can clear it.
  • Your notification preferences and payment-request settings.
  • On your own device only (browser storage, never sent to us): your chosen currency, cached exchange rates, and whether we've already asked you to turn on notifications.
  • Server logs from our Cloud Functions record user IDs, what action ran and how many notifications were delivered, for debugging and abuse prevention.

Ads and tips

  • iPhone app ads: Google AdMob collects device and usage information to show the banner ad. If you allow tracking, it may also use your device's advertising identifier. See Ads and tracking.
  • Website ads: Google AdSense shows ads on the website's login page and at the bottom of your list, and may use cookies. AdSense is not loaded in the iPhone app.
  • Tips: the "Buy us a coffee" tip in the iPhone app is an in-app purchase processed entirely by Apple. We receive no card details, name or email, only Apple's aggregate sales reports. On the website, tips go through Buy Me a Coffee on its own site.

What we don't collect

TABS does not use any analytics or crash-reporting tools. It does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location. "Invite to TABS" opens your phone's own share sheet or messaging app; we don't see who you send it to. If you use Continue as Guest, nothing you enter is saved to our servers and it is gone when you leave the page.

2. How we use it

  • To run TABS: sign you in, save and sync your tabs across devices, connect you with friends, deliver reminders and notifications, and build payment links.
  • To keep people safe: enforce blocks, review reports, rate-limit reminders and requests, and suspend accounts that break our Terms.
  • To support you: answer emails and feedback, and fix bugs.
  • To keep TABS free: show ads (see Ads and tracking) and offer an optional tip.
  • To meet legal obligations and respond to lawful requests.

We never use your tabs, notes, friends or payment handles for advertising, and we never give them to ad networks. We don't make automated decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.

If you are in the EEA or UK, our legal bases are: performing our contract with you (running the service); our legitimate interests in keeping TABS safe, secure and free (safety features, logs, non-personalized ads); your consent (personalized ads, push notifications); and legal obligations.

3. What other people can see

  • Anyone who has your friend code can look up your first and last name, so they can confirm it's you before sending a request.
  • Someone you send a friend request to sees your name and friend code.
  • Confirmed friends can see your name, friend code, phone number (if you gave one) and any payment handles you added. They cannot see your email address, your list of tabs, your notes, your other friends or your account history.
  • A friend you remind receives a notification with your name and the reminder message, which includes the amount.
  • People you track who don't use TABS see nothing. TABS never contacts them.
  • Blocking is silent. The blocked person is not told, and a friend request they try to send you looks sent on their side but never reaches you. Reports are confidential: we don't tell the reported person who reported them.

5. Ads and tracking

  • iPhone app. TABS shows one Google AdMob banner at the bottom of the screen. In the EEA and UK, Google's consent form asks for your choice first. Then iOS asks whether TABS may track you (App Tracking Transparency). If you allow tracking, AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) to show personalized ads and measure them. If you don't, the identifier is not shared and ads are not personalized with cross-app tracking. AdMob still receives basic device information such as model, OS version, IP address and ad interactions so it can serve and count ads.
  • Website. When you use tabsonfriends.com in a browser, Google AdSense may set cookies to show and measure ads. Visitors in the EEA and UK are asked for consent by Google's consent message. The iPhone app never loads AdSense; its only ads are the AdMob banner above.
  • We never send your name, email, phone number, tabs or friends to any ad network.
  • We don't sell personal information for money. Some US state laws treat personalized advertising that uses an advertising identifier as "sharing". You can opt out at any time by turning tracking off (see Your choices).

6. Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account exists. You can clear your notifications, account history and list of tabs yourself at any time.

Deleting your account

In TABS, open the menu, then Profile → Account → Delete Account. When you confirm, our server immediately and permanently:

  • deletes everything stored under your account: profile, tabs, notes, friends list, friend requests, notifications, account history, settings, payment handles, push tokens and block list;
  • deletes the delivery-log entries for messages you sent, and for messages your friends and pending requests sent you;
  • releases your friend code;
  • removes you from your friends' lists, their pending and sent requests, and their auto-reminder settings;
  • deletes your sign-in account from Firebase Authentication.

If you signed in with Apple, TABS first asks you to confirm with Apple and then revokes TABS's access to your Apple ID. Deletion cannot be undone.

What stays after deletion

  • Notifications you already sent to other people stay in their inbox until they clear them, and information other users added to their own account themselves is theirs.
  • If someone blocked you, your first and last name and the date they blocked you stay in their private block list so the block keeps working. Only they can see it.
  • Reports you made or that were made about you are kept as long as needed to handle the safety issue, and we review and delete old reports at least once a year.
  • Delivery-log entries for messages someone sent you before they stopped being your friend stay (at most the last 20) until that person deletes their account.
  • Rate-limit records (two user IDs and a timestamp) may remain.
  • Server logs are kept by Google Cloud Logging for up to 30 days. Google may keep backup copies for a limited time under its own policies.

Disabling instead of deleting

Disable Account (also in Profile → Account) stops anyone from signing in to your account but keeps your data. Email us to re-enable it or to have it deleted.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Get a copy: Profile → Account → Export Data gives you a JSON file of everything stored under your account. On the website it downloads the file; in the iPhone app it opens the share sheet so you can save it to Files or send it. You can also email us and we'll send you a copy.
  • Correct: edit your tabs and payment handles in the app. For profile details you can't edit in the app, email us.
  • Delete: Profile → Account → Delete Account (see above), or email us.
  • Notifications: Profile → Account → Manage Notifications (muted types are not pushed to your devices and are hidden in your TABS inbox), or turn TABS off in iPhone Settings → Notifications, or in your browser's site settings.
  • Tracking: iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → TABS. You can also reset your advertising identifier in iPhone settings, and manage Google ad personalization at myadcenter.google.com.
  • Ad consent (EEA/UK): in the iPhone app, open the menu and tap Ad privacy choices to change or withdraw your answer to Google's consent form at any time. It appears when your region requires it. You can also email us.
  • Block and report: block anyone who bothers you, and report abuse. See Support.

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or US state privacy laws), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. Email us to use any of these rights; we reply within 30 days and will not treat you differently for using them. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

8. Security

All traffic to TABS uses HTTPS. Database security rules let each account read and write only its own data, plus the few friend-only fields described above, and our server checks friendships and blocks before it sends any notification. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password and tell us right away if you think your account was misused.

9. Children

TABS is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

10. International transfers

TABS is run from the United States, and our providers store and process data in the United States and other countries where they operate. If you use TABS from outside the US, your information is transferred to the US, where privacy laws may differ from yours. Our providers protect these transfers with safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top. If a change materially affects how we use your information, we will also tell you in TABS before it takes effect.
